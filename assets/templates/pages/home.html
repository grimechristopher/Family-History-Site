Why we’re doing this

None of this is written down anywhere. What you remember is the only copy, and once it’s gone it’s gone.

It’s for the grandkids, and for their kids after them. And honestly it’s for me — I never met most of the people in these questions, and this is how I get to know them.

How to answer

  • Go on as long as you like. Longer is better. There is no such thing as too much.
  • Wander off the question. If you start on one thing and remember another, put it all in the same answer. I would much rather have it than have it tidy.
  • Don’t decide what’s worth keeping. Say it anyway, even if you’re not sure it matters. The small things are usually the ones we’d never have known otherwise.
  • Nothing has to be finished. It saves as you go, so you can stop halfway and come back.
